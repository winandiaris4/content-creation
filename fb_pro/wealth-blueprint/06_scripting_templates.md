# 06. Scripting Templates & Hooks — Wealth Blueprint

Dokumen ini memuat arsitektur penulisan naskah (*scripting framework*), bank formula hook, taktik retensi Reels, serta struktur Call-to-Action (CTA) berkonversi tinggi.

---

## 1. Arsitektur CTA Tiga Titik (*3-Point CTA Model*)

Ajakan bertindak (*Call to Action*) yang generik seperti *"Please like, share, and follow"* tidak efektif karena penonton mengabaikannya. Wealth Blueprint menggunakan 3 titik penempatan strategis:

```
[00:15 - 00:30]                   [01:30 - 02:00]                   [02:45 - 03:00]
  Hook CTA                          Mid-Roll Reminder                 Closing Benefit CTA
  "Tahan sampai akhir..."           "Inilah blueprint mingguan..."    "Follow & nantikan edisi..."
```

### Titik 1: Hook CTA (Detik 15–30) — Menahan Penonton
Tujuannya bukan meminta follow, melainkan mengunci ekspektasi penonton agar tidak *swipe away*:
> *"Stay until the end — I’ll show you the exact 3-step calculation from this book that banks never show on their websites."*

### Titik 2: Mid-Roll Reminder (Detik 90–120) — Penanaman Brand
Disisipkan secara halus di sela-sela pergantian poin solusi:
> *"This is the exact kind of framework we deconstruct every week here on Wealth Blueprint."*

### Titik 3: Closing Benefit CTA (15 Detik Terakhir) — Menjual Manfaat Konten
Formula utama: **Jual manfaat follow dan buat antisipasi untuk episode berikutnya**, bukan sekadar meminta tindakan follow.
* ❌ *CTA Lemah:* "That's it for today! Don't forget to like, comment, and follow our page!"
* ✅ *CTA Kuat:* *"If this changed how you view your credit card interest, follow Wealth Blueprint. Next Thursday, we’re breaking down the single chapter from The Millionaire Fastlane that changed how millionaires build cash flow."*

## 2. Taktik Khusus Facebook Reels

Pada format pendek vertikal (Reels), rentang perhatian penonton sangat sempit. Terapkan 3 taktik berikut:

1. **Loop Bait (Video Berputar Sempurna):**
   * Hubungkan kalimat penutup video secara mulus ke kalimat pembuka di awal video.
   * *Contoh Akhir:* "...and that is why you should never trust..."
   * *Contoh Awal:* "...the standard advice given by commercial banks."
   * Penonton yang tidak sadar video telah selesai akan menonton ulang 2–3 detik pertama, meningkatkan retensi di atas 100%.

2. **Comment Bait (Pemicu Algoritma Terkuat):**
   Komentar memiliki bobot algoritma jauh lebih tinggi daripada sekadar like.
   > *"Comment 'BLUEPRINT' below and I'll send you the exact debt-payoff calculation template for free."*

3. **Text Overlay 3 Detik Terakhir:**
   Sematkan banner teks visual sederhana di akhir video tanpa memotong alur narasi:
   `[ Follow @WealthBlueprint for Weekly Money Frameworks ]`

## 3. Bank Tipe Hook (Hook Archetypes)

Gunakan rotasi 3 tipe hook secara bergantian untuk menjangkau tipe psikologi audiens yang berbeda:

### Tipe 1: The Stat Hook (Berbasis Data & Shock Value)
Menargetkan audiens logis dan berbasis angka:
* *"Most Americans lose $500,000 in retirement because of THIS one hidden fee — and it takes 10 minutes to fix."*
* *"93% of first-time stock investors lose money in their first 12 months. Here is what the top 7% do completely differently."*
* *"Over 60% of people earning six figures still live paycheck to paycheck. The reason comes down to one psychological trap."*

### Tipe 2: The Question / Pain Hook (Menyentuh Masalah Emosional)
Menargetkan rasa cemas atau rasa penasaran:
* *"Why does your credit score actually DROP right after you pay off a major debt?"*
* *"Are you trapped in the minimum payment cycle without even realizing it?"*
* *"What would happen to your wealth if you invested $100 a month starting at age 20 vs age 30? The math will shock you."*

### Tipe 3: The Reveal / Contrarian Hook (Membongkar Mitos)
Menantang persepsi umum untuk memicu debat dan share:
* *"What Rich Dad Poor Dad got dangerously wrong about owning your own home."*
* *"Why putting your emergency fund in a regular savings account is secretly burning your wealth."*
* *"The 'Latte Factor' is a myth. Here is the real math on why cutting coffee won't make you a millionaire."*

## 4. Master Template Naskah Video 3 Menit (In-Stream)

Gunakan struktur berikut untuk menulis draf naskah video reguler:

```markdown
### Judul Naskah: [Judul Konten Berisi Angka & Hook]
**Pilar:** [Debt & Credit / Wealth Psychology / dll.]
**Buku / Framework Referensi:** [Nama Buku & Penulis]
**BGM Style:** [Cinematic Tension -> Corporate Uplifting]

---

#### [00:00 - 00:30] SEGMENT 1: THE HOOK & THE PAIN
- **Visual Animasi:** [Deskripsi visual dramatis, kontras pekat, teks judul tebal]
- **Voiceover Narasi:** [3 kalimat pertama: Stat/Question Hook + Agitasi masalah nyata audiens]
- **On-Screen Text:** [Teks kunci penguat hook]

#### [00:30 - 01:15] SEGMENT 2: THE MISCONCEPTION
- **Visual Animasi:** [Ilustrasi orang terjebak cara lama/kesalahan umum]
- **Voiceover Narasi:** [Jelaskan mengapa solusi konvensional gagal atau merugikan]
- **Hook CTA (Detik 25):** "Stay with me for the next 2 minutes to see the exact formula..."

#### [01:15 - 02:15] SEGMENT 3: THE BLUEPRINT DECONSTRUCTION
- **Visual Animasi:** [Transisi ke BGM Corporate Uplifting, diagram langkah 1, 2, 3]
- **Voiceover Narasi:** [Penyampaian inti framework buku secara sederhana, runtut, padat]
- **Mid-Roll Reminder (Detik 100):** [Branding halus Wealth Blueprint]

#### [02:15 - 02:45] SEGMENT 4: THE "SO WHAT" & ACTION STEPS
- **Visual Animasi:** [Simulasi hasil nyata, grafik kenaikan finansial atau penghematan hutang]
- **Voiceover Narasi:** [Langkah aksi konkret yang bisa dieksekusi hari ini oleh penonton]

#### [02:45 - 03:00] SEGMENT 5: CLOSING & TEASER CTA
- **Visual Animasi:** [Kartu outro warna Navy #080E1F & Gold #C99E43]
- **Voiceover Narasi:** "If this blueprint helped you today, follow Wealth Blueprint. Next Tuesday: [Teaser video berikutnya]..."
```
